    One Pickle, Many Delicious Ways

    Perfect with Hot Rice

    Mix a spoonful of pickle into steaming hot rice with a little ghee. The warmth brings out the spices, and it makes a simple, comforting meal.

    Side with Dosa & Idli

    Serve a podi or pickle on the side with crispy dosas or soft idlis. A little spice gives an everyday breakfast that Andhra and Telangana kick.

    Spread on Roti & Paratha

    Spread a thin layer of gongura or tomato pickle on warm roti or paratha for a quick, flavour-packed meal.

    Flavour Boost for Curries

    Stir a small spoonful of pickle into dals or stir-fries for an extra spicy, tangy depth of flavour.
